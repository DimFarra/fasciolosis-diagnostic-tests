# Coprology prior assumptions

The baseline herd sensitivity prior is Beta(4.842, 3.561), with mode approximately 0.60, probability 0.95 above 0.30, and equal-tailed 95% interval 0.253–0.865. The specificity prior is Beta(14.844, 4.461), with mode approximately 0.80, probability 0.95 above 0.60, and equal-tailed 95% interval 0.563–0.923.

These distributions are modelling assumptions rather than pooled literature estimates or a documented formal expert elicitation. Relevant coprological studies provide diagnostic context but do not establish a quantitative derivation of these Beta shapes.

Their influence is assessed using widened Beta priors, Beta(2,2) priors and uniform Beta(1,1) test priors. Uniform denotes the probability scale; joint model constraints can alter the effective marginal priors. Slaughterhouse baseline ELISA and coprology priors are obtained by moment matching the primary herd posterior distributions. Alternative test-prior scenarios replace these transferred distributions.

Conditional-independence models are primary; conditional-dependence models are structural sensitivity analyses. Posterior estimates and credible intervals should be interpreted alongside the alternative-prior results.
